About this page

Each surface observation is paired with the nearest model grid point (max 0.5° distance accepted). AROME Arctic 2.5 km (00 Z and 12 Z, hourly steps, 66 h), IFS HRES 0.1° (00 Z and 12 Z, hourly steps, 72 h), AIFS 0.1° (00 Z and 12 Z, 6-hourly steps, 72 h), IFS experimental 0.1° (00 Z and 12 Z, hourly steps, 72 h, expver 80). ECMWF models downloaded on 0.1° regular grid (closest to the IFS native O1280 ≈ 9 km resolution). BIAS = mean(model−obs) — positive means model too high. MAE = mean absolute error. RMSE = root-mean-square error. Statistics updated daily.
